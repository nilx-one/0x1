# 0x1 Product Identity and Trademark Policy

**Status:** initial policy; it does not claim that every listed mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

Source-code or documentation licenses do not grant permission to present a derivative product as official 0x1.

## Protected Project Identity

The project reserves control over product identity associated with:

- `0x1`;
- `nilx.one`;
- `nilx-one` where used as project or organization identity;
- official 0x1 and nilx.one logos, emblems, wordmarks, and visual identity;
- confusingly similar names or presentation that imply official status, sponsorship, endorsement, or canonical compatibility.

## Allowed Referential Use

Truthful referential statements are allowed where applicable law permits them. Examples include saying that software interoperates with 0x1, is derived from published 0x1 source, or is being evaluated against 0x1.

Referential use must not imply that a third-party product is maintained, approved, sponsored, or operated by the canonical project unless that relationship actually exists.

## Forks and Derivatives

A fork or derivative that is permitted by its applicable source license must use its own product identity unless the project steward has granted explicit permission otherwise.

Changing protocol semantics and continuing to market the result as canonical `0x1` is not permitted by this identity policy.

A derivative must not reuse official logos or presentation in a way that makes users reasonably believe they are using the canonical application or service.

## Compatibility Language

Claims such as `0x1-compatible`, `0x1-conformant`, `official 0x1 client`, or equivalent are governed by [CONFORMANCE.md](CONFORMANCE.md) and any later certification or trademark program.

Passing a technical conformance suite does not by itself grant a right to use protected project identity as a product name.

## Permissions

The project may grant written permission for integrations, distributions, events, research, partnerships, or other uses. Permission may be scoped and may be revoked or revised according to its terms.
