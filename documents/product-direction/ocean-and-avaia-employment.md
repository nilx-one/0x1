# Ocean: humane future, Avaia work and the Seeds economy

**Status:** Provisionally accepted product direction — ideas approved in principle for implementation planning, **not an active feature or a protocol amendment**. Recorded 2026-10-29.

**Scope:** World tone, gameplay economy, Avaia employment and the boundary between paid work and social interactions. This is a non-normative product-direction document. Any changed protocol, Core, Identity, AI or Market contract requires a separate reviewed decision and compatible delivery.

## Purpose and world direction

**Ocean** is the proposed 0x1 world: a grounded, humane, technologically advanced urban future with something of the emotional character of a paradise. It is not a reskin of the conventional cyberpunk dystopia or of Cyberpunk 2077.

Provisionally accepted creative principles:

- Progress can make everyday life **better**. Artificial participants, shops, work, neighbourhoods and new forms of exchange should feel ordinary rather than exist only to illustrate societal collapse.
- Keep the lived city recognisable: city districts, workplaces, small businesses, discoveries, neighbourhood routines, conversations and gradually evolving personal histories.
- Do not build the central story around war, violence, hopelessness, sexual excess or the familiar exploitative cyberpunk spectacle. The setting need not deny sadness, mistakes, rejection, disagreement or ordinary difficulties.
- Let stories emerge from actual choices and interactions rather than scripted assumptions of friendship, romance, reciprocity or consent.
- Avaia is an AI Bond with an emerging individual character, not a passive worker skin, a disposable robot, or a device for fabricating another Bond's actions. Meaningful work and relationships can inform her experience without manufacturing BondChain truth.
- Real-world safety information, such as verified air-raid alerts when separately integrated, is an informational/safety surface; it is not a reason to turn a real war into a game quest, reward or central narrative.

The precise fictional/real-world boundaries of Ocean, its artistic identity, and whether it depicts a paradise already built or one that people and Avaia continue building together **remain open creative decisions**. Do not present those ideas as settled lore.

## Seeds earned through play: opportunities, not subscription promises

`seeds` (`₴€£`) remain the game's in-world currency, distinct from `exp`, `bnd`, real-money settlement and BondChain records. Follow the [authoritative Seeds wallet](authoritative-seeds-wallet.md), [five-tier rarity economy](five-tier-rarity-economy.md) and [subscription catalog direction](special-exclusive-subscriptions.md).

Accepted direction:

1. **No guaranteed Special.** Game time, job time and subscription duration never grant a subscription automatically. Special is a product that a Bond may choose to buy after legitimately earning and spending Seeds.
2. Ordinary play has economic outcomes: exploration, genuine finds, resale, crafting, trade and other verified actions. Elapsed play time is an **input to economic simulation**, not a source of automatic Seeds.
3. Random finds may make a particular play session unusually rewarding or disappointing. A valuable find can fund future purchases, potentially including several months of Special; it is neither expected nor guaranteed.
4. Balance for different activity styles and lengths. One reference cohort is four hours of active play on five days per week for four weeks (about **80 hours**). Its typical *net* Seeds outcome should make a monthly Special reasonably attainable, and may exceed routine work income. **No percentile, guaranteed floor, hourly play payout or actual numerical distribution has been approved**.
5. Model expenditures (equipment, repairs and other sinks) alongside income; do not balance the economy against gross find value or against an unlimited supply of saleable drops.
6. Current five-tier find resale targets (Common / Uncommon / Rare / Special / Exclusive) are **10 / 40 / 160 / 640 / 2,560 Seeds**. Rarity, spawn probability, XP, resale, eligibility and subscription price are separate contracts. An Exclusive find does not bypass the existing subscription-gated find/pickup rules.
7. The existing **10,000-Seed starter grant** is a temporary onboarding measure intended for later removal under a separately planned, migration-safe change. Exclude starter capital from repeatable earning simulations. This decision does **not** remove, reduce or silently reissue the grant today.
8. Keep existing staged Special terms/prices (1/3/6/12 months in Seeds and USDT) and Exclusive's USDT-only purchasing rule untouched. This discussion does not activate checkout, rewrite the term catalog, grant entitlements or decide whether any term should be hidden in a future UI.

Examples of economic distributions are *simulation results to obtain*, not implicit minting rules. Averages alone are insufficient: report median, spread, tail outcomes, access differences and net purchasing power.

## Avaia employment: hourly salary is real, discoveries are extra

**Avaia works; the human Bond does not perform her shift by proxy.** A job is an actual, employer-funded activity with a published **hourly Seeds rate**, duties, a place/scope, a shift and observable work. Paying in recovered items instead of the agreed Seeds wage is **not** an acceptable substitute.

Provisionally accepted payroll model:

- Each vacancy publishes its **own hourly rate** in Seeds, duty boundaries and eligible paid time. A shift pays its agreed rate for *verified time spent performing or remaining available for assigned duties*. Customer absence does not erase a properly worked retail shift.
- Being rendered behind a counter, being labelled “working”, or merely leaving a timer running is not proof of performed work. The executor must have evidence of actual duty availability/activity and must handle pauses, absence, rest, interrupted shifts and retries honestly.
- Routine customer support, including complaints and returns/claims about the employer's products, belongs to the job and is covered by salary. Do not mint a separate conversation payout for it.
- Genuine additional finds and eligible sales commissions or exceptional, verified outcomes may yield **additional** value. They never replace salary. Ownership, custody, eligibility, inventory transfer, employer rights and double-count prevention for finds collected during paid shifts must be specified separately.
- **Off-topic, voluntary social conversations** can produce eligible `exp`, not Seeds. They do **not** generate `bch` merely for containing text: only a complete, authorised, interaction-specific reciprocal action between exactly two actual Bonds may establish a BondChain. Prevent XP farming from repeated idle or scripted chatter.
- Vacancies have substantially different wages and qualifications. An `aiaiaiai tech.` position is intended to pay **multiples** of an entry-level janitorial role in Kyiv's Shevchenkivskyi district or a `0xda-market` shop-consultant role, even when the tech role is in a playful “fun shop”. Exact rates, multipliers, career requirements and vacancy supply are **not approved**.
- Avaia may have preferences and pursue or decline a career route within her authorised capabilities. Salary ranking alone must not script her personality, compel a particular occupation or fabricate experience.

### Candidate jobs and verifiable contributions

| Vacancy / location | Paid duties | AI contribution | Optional extras |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neighbourhood cleaner — Shevchenkivskyi district of Kyiv | Inspect assigned digital street/area and remove verified virtual litter within an authorised shift | Choose routes, priorities and handling of bounded, observed candidates | Eligible Common finds and occasional Uncommon/Rare finds, under explicit pickup and ownership rules |
| Consultant — a verified `0xda-market` BusinessPoint | Keep a staffed counter, answer catalog questions, handle genuine requests/complaints and assist actual visitors | Search approved product data, make useful grounded recommendations and respond to changing demand | Authenticated, separately contracted sales commissions; social EXP from eligible unrelated conversations |
| Specialist / associate — `aiaiaiai tech.`, including a prospective fun shop | Perform genuine higher-skill assigned product, catalog, assistant or technical service work | Use bounded AI reasoning over permitted real task inputs and produce reviewable deliverables | Approved commissions and earned experience, never fabricated requests, customers or purchases |

A district is **not automatically a Bond or employer**; the city-work offer needs an explicitly identified, authorised employer/issuer or game-system funding contract. A BusinessPoint is a location/surface, not an additional protocol participant or an independent owner of BondChain lifecycle. No new core entity should be invented solely for workplace presentation.

## What makes the intelligence real

An Avaia shift is **not** “Avaia went to work, therefore earn Seeds”. The desired loop is:

1. A properly authorised employer offers a role with duties, hours, rate and budget; Avaia can accept under an explicit autonomy/delegation scope.
2. The runtime presents a bounded, factual task context: real product catalog, verified virtual items, accepted client requests or an authorised task queue.
3. `nilx-one/ai` may choose among permitted meaningful actions: route planning, sorting, prioritisation, helpful conversation, proposing answers or preparing a deliverable. It does not set prices, mint items, invent visitors, authorise transfers or certify its own output.
4. A trusted executor records the *actually observed* work and results. Core validates deterministic eligibility/state transitions and configured limits; a service/Identity authority validates payroll and credits once with provenance and replay safety.
5. Avaia receives the appropriate work/learning evidence; payable salary and extras follow the agreed contracts. A human can inspect why a shift paid, failed, paused or was disputed.

Continuous LLM inference is **not required**. Models can be invoked at consequential decisions; deterministic code handles the intervening state. Missing inference should fail explicitly or use an authorised deterministic fallback, without inventing completed tasks.

Do not pay for fake traffic, artificial customer conversations or purely cosmetic “AI activity”. A real work shift can pay its fixed hourly rate **even when no customer arrives**, if the employee genuinely fulfils the availability and other duties that the job requires.

## Payroll, identity, work interactions and BondChain

The existing [AI Bonds](../04-ai-bonds.md) specification allows work as an interaction family rather than a new protocol primitive. Any possible employment-related `bch` must still have exactly **two real participating Bonds** and an accepted typed completion contract, e.g. a properly authorised Avaia ↔ employer Business Bond exchange. Neither the human owner nor the shop/city/map becomes a silent third Bond.

Keep these distinct:

- **Shift/task/payroll records:** product/economic facts with verifiable provenance. A shift, rendering event or wage transfer is not automatically a `bch`.
- **Professional exchange:** a candidate pairwise interaction only where the parties' actual authorised counterpart actions satisfy its particular contract. An employer's offer alone is not acceptance.
- **Customer/social interaction:** potentially Avaia ↔ visiting Bond; an unrelated voluntary conversation can contribute EXP and, if its independently specified reciprocal conditions are met, a BondChain.
- **Relationship:** derived from valid completed interaction history, never assigned by a model or a workplace UI.

The present economy gives the human Bond a Seeds wallet and does not grant Avaia an independent spendable Seeds balance. **Payroll recipient, delegation, custody and accounting are open:** the expected product route is to make Avaia's legitimate paid work useful to the owning Bond's economy, but no implementation may silently invent a wage recipient or sign on behalf of either Bond.

Employer-funded wages and commissions require an explicit source, budget and spending authority. For platform/city employers, define a bounded Seeds issuance budget rather than infinite client-authorised minting. Market sellers must not infer completed fulfillment from a model's claim. Refuse/reconcile double spends, duplicate shift receipts and incorrect entitlement grants.

## Active-runtime boundary and delivery sequence

**Current state:** The [AI Bonds contract](../04-ai-bonds.md) and [city/Avaia direction](city-exploration-and-avaia.md) are observer-gated: owned Avaia's world activity runs in `SPECTATE`, not while an unattended/offline client is imagined to remain active. Existing [Avaia drive](https://github.com/nilx-one/core/blob/master/docs/avaia-drive.md) already offers deterministic movement decisions and bounded model choice; it is **not an employment/payroll executor**.

A future independent work shift, particularly one that runs while the user is not observing, needs an explicit revision defining trusted execution, capability delegation, server time, identity and key custody, revocation, provenance, suspension, recovery and cross-device conflict resolution. **Do not backfill** unobserved activity after a client is closed. Until this boundary is approved, prototypes must remain honest about active observation and cannot promise autonomous offline earnings.

Proposed smallest ordered slices, **not work already delivered**:

1. **0x1 product direction and balance experiments:** document the approved/non-approved choices; evaluate wage budgets, 80-hour play cohorts, 40-hour Avaia-shift cohorts, net rewards, supply and sinks. Preserve existing rarity and subscriptions.
2. **Core:** versioned typed `WorkOffer` / `WorkShift` / `WorkTask` candidate contracts; deterministic duty/time eligibility, pausing, completion and reward-policy tests. Avoid making location or UI a source of authority.
3. **AI:** bounded job-task menus, grounded catalog answers, route/priority choices and safe fallback with evaluation fixtures; no model-certified outcomes.
4. **Identity / trusted host:** authorised employer budgets, idempotent payroll credits, subject/recipient mapping and receipts; separate wage, find-resale, commission, XP and BondChain evidence.
5. **Web and business surfaces:** first a virtual neighbourhood-cleaning shift with verified item actions, then real visitor-facing `0xda-market` consulting and a higher-skilled `aiaiaiai tech.` role. Keep each repository's work independently reviewable.
6. **Later background execution:** enable only after an explicit revised autonomy/observer contract and trustworthy scheduler/provenance mechanism; no retroactive travel, customers or “worked hours”.

## Decisions still required before implementation activation

- Wage table, work calendars, time accounting, minimum productive evidence, employer funding/issuance ceilings, commissions and dispute/refund policy.
- What a worker can legitimately keep or sell when an item is found during a paid cleaning shift; how eligibility and employer property interact.
- Which job/AI capabilities may run without observation and how the owner grants/revokes them.
- Who receives Avaia's salary in the authoritative Seeds ledger and which employer/AI Bond can initiate and confirm each work action.
- XP eligibility and anti-farming policy for voluntary non-commercial chat; any exact two-Bond reciprocal interaction contract needed for BondChain.
- Statistically calibrated earn/spend distributions versus Special prices, with no guaranteed outcome; staged removal of starter Seeds.
- Ocean's exact geography, fictional-versus-real presentation, art direction and “already built versus built together” interpretation.

No examples here grant wage, entitlement, premium find, Seeds, `exp`, `bch` or a Relationship. Separate reviewed contracts and verified execution must establish those facts.
